# Launch with Thai songs first

The channel launches translating **Thai** Source Songs into English, with the
Japanese **Vocaloid** scene as the planned fast-follow.

## Why

The legal-first catalog (ADR-0001) lives or dies on *getting permission from
indie artists* — a relationship game. Thailand gives home-field advantage:
language, knowledge of the scene, and the ability to actually reach อินดี้
artists and small labels for a yes. The Thai→English niche is also near-empty.

## Considered options

- **Japan (J-pop / anime) first** — much larger global audience, but JASRAC
  enforcement and restrictive labels make clearance hard, and the
  Japanese→English cover niche is already saturated. Rejected for launch.
- **Japan via Vocaloid** — the Vocaloid / Hatsune Miku scene has a
  derivative-friendly, often Creative-Commons culture, making it genuinely
  clearable. Kept as the fast-follow once the Thai model works.
- **Thai first (chosen)** — clearable supply we can personally negotiate, low
  competition, lower audience ceiling at the start.

## Consequences

- Lower initial audience ceiling than a Japan launch, accepted in exchange for a
  catalog we can actually clear and a niche we can own.
