# Curated flagship launch, not broad outreach

We seed the catalog by hand-picking **3–5 Anchor Partners** — respected Thai
indie artists whose taste defines the channel — and signing them first, rather
than mass-outreaching many artists for volume or waiting for open submissions.

## Why

Legal-first (ADR-0001) and a signature singer (ADR-0004) already trade reach for
identity; the seeding strategy compounds that choice. The first Partner Artists
do double duty: their vibe *defines* the channel's taste, and they become the
social proof that makes the next wave of artists say yes. A coherent handful of
respected names gives the Signature Singer a focused body of work and gives
later outreach real credibility.

## Considered options

- **Broad outreach** — mass-contact artists, take whoever says yes. Fills the
  catalog faster, but weak curation risks off-brand songs diluting the channel
  before its identity is set. Rejected at launch.
- **Open submissions** — let artists submit their songs. Zero outreach cost, but
  needs reach we don't have yet — chicken-and-egg. Deferred until the channel has
  an audience.
- **Curated flagship launch (chosen)** — slower to fill, but coherent identity
  and strong credibility.

## Consequences

- Catalog grows slowly at first — accepted, and well-matched to the signature
  singer's bounded throughput (ADR-0004).
- Landing the first Anchor Partners is a concentrated bet: a few key "yes"es
  gate the launch, so early outreach effort focuses on a short, hand-picked list.
- Once anchors are live, they become the pitch for broader outreach or open
  submissions later.
