# Song Bridge

A YouTube channel that takes songs from one culture and re-releases them as
faithful English-singable versions — same melody, a sung English performance —
built on a catalog the channel is legally cleared to translate.

## Language

**Source Song**:
The original, foreign-language recording a video is based on. The thing we
translate _from_.
_Avoid_: Original track, master (— "master" means something specific, below)

**Cover**:
A video we produce: our own re-created instrumental plus a sung English vocal.
A Cover never reuses the Source Song's original recording. Producing a Cover
clears the _master_ problem but not the _adaptation_ problem.
_Avoid_: Remix, reupload, karaoke

**Singable Adaptation**:
English lyrics rewritten to fit the original melody, stress, and rhyme so they
can be sung over the tune. Trades literal accuracy for singability. This — not a
literal translation — is the product.
_Avoid_: Translation (too vague — see Literal Gloss), subtitle, lyric

**Literal Gloss**:
A plain, meaning-accurate English rendering of the original lyrics, shown as a
secondary aid (e.g. a pinned comment). Never sung.
_Avoid_: Translation, subtitle

**Cover Voice**:
The sung English vocal on a Cover, performed by the channel's Signature Singer.
A faithful performance, deliberately _not_ an identity-clone of the Source Song's
artist.
_Avoid_: AI voice, clone, soundalike

**Signature Singer**:
The channel's own recurring human vocalist — one person or a small fixed roster —
who performs the Cover Voice across every video. The channel's recognizable
identity _is_ this voice, rather than a match to each Source Song's artist.
_Avoid_: Host, talent, narrator

**Adaptation Right**:
The rights-holder's control over translating or altering a song's lyrics. A
separate permission from the cover/mechanical licence, and the gate every video
must pass. Held by the publisher/songwriter.
_Avoid_: Cover licence, sync licence

**Master**:
The copyright in the Source Song's original sound recording, owned by the
label. We never use it; re-creating the instrumental is how we avoid it.
_Avoid_: Original, recording rights

**Clearable Song**:
A Source Song we are allowed to adapt — because it is public domain, Creative
Commons, or its artist/publisher has personally granted Adaptation Right. The
launch catalog is made only of these.
_Avoid_: Licensed song, available song

**Legal-First Catalog**:
The selection rule: only Clearable Songs ship. Famous label hits are excluded
until their Adaptation Right is bought. Identity over reach.
_Avoid_: Whitelist, approved list

**Partner Artist**:
An indie artist who has granted us the Adaptation Right and instrumental stems
for one of their songs under a Revenue-Share Partnership. The main source of the
launch catalog.
_Avoid_: Licensor, rights-holder, client

**Revenue-Share Partnership**:
The standard deal with a Partner Artist: we split the video's revenue and
credit/link their original; they grant the Adaptation Right and stems.
Incentive-aligned and self-marketing (the artist re-shares the video).
_Avoid_: Licence, contract, deal
